Common symptoms

Power quality problems rarely announce themselves. They show up as failures, trips and heat that nobody can explain. If any of these sound familiar, measurement is the place to start.

  • Nuisance tripping. Breakers and earth leakage devices that trip without a clear fault.
  • Overheating cables or transformers. Harmonic currents and unbalance add heat without doing useful work.
  • Failed capacitors. Power factor correction capacitors that fail early, often through resonance with existing harmonics.
  • Damaged electronics. Drives, power supplies and controllers that fail after voltage dips or swells.
  • Flickering lighting. Rapid voltage fluctuations from large motor starts, welding or a weak supply.
  • Unexplained motor failures. Voltage unbalance and harmonics raise motor temperatures and shorten winding life.
  • Disputes with the utility about supply quality. You need measured evidence of the voltage you receive.

What the investigation covers

  1. Simultaneous logging at several distribution boards. We log the main incomer and the affected boards at the same time, so causes and effects line up.
  2. Root-cause analysis and identification of problem loads. We trace harmonics, unbalance and voltage events to the loads or supply conditions that cause them.
  3. Transformer and cable derating assessment. We check whether harmonic currents and unbalance mean transformers and cables must carry less load than their rating.
  4. Vendor-neutral specification for harmonic filters or detuned power factor correction. A specification based on your measured conditions, so any supplier can quote against it.
  5. A formal supply quality report suitable for submission to the utility. Voltage quality assessed against NRS 048-2 from a measurement campaign designed for that purpose, and customer emissions assessed against NRS 048-4 where required.

How it relates to the Energy Loss Audit

Every Energy Loss Audit includes a Power Quality Screening at the main incomer. The screening shows whether a problem exists and how serious it is. The investigation often follows when the screening rates a parameter as Action, but you can also appoint it on its own, without an audit.
