The problem

Contractors often commit to a project before the full engineering, operational, regulatory and standards picture has been worked out. The contractor is focused on its contractual scope. The owner carries the consequences: Unclear requirements, undocumented assumptions, unsafe interfaces, untested systems, incomplete records and defects found only after handover.

Where we fit

Your engineering, operations or facilities manager answers for the outcome but rarely has the time, the specialist depth or the independence to challenge every submission, test record and variation. We make the technical position visible before an avoidable failure becomes a safety, operational, contractual or financial problem.

What an assurance review is, and isn't

It is an objective engineering opinion within an agreed scope and verified competence. It is not a statutory approval, a Certificate of Compliance, an approved inspection authority's report or a guarantee of legal compliance. The designer, contractor, registered electrical contractor, CoC-issuing registered person, inspection authority, distributor, municipality and regulator keep their responsibilities. We help you get ready for those processes.

Independent by design

On an assurance mandate we don't supply equipment, install systems or take undisclosed supplier commissions. We disclose relevant interests, keep controlled records of everything reviewed, observed and witnessed, and tie every finding to its evidence.

Compliance assurance questions

What is an owner's engineer?

An independent engineer who works for the asset owner, not the contractor or supplier. We review requirements, tenders, delivery evidence and handover packs so the owner can approve, challenge, defer or accept on evidence.

Do you issue Certificates of Compliance?

No. An assurance review is advisory. The registered electrical contractor and CoC-issuing registered person keep that responsibility. We help you check that the evidence for those processes is complete and credible.

Can you witness factory and site acceptance tests?

Yes. Where our appointment includes it, we witness FAT and SAT, review commissioning and test records, and track non-conformities and missing evidence to close-out.

When should we appoint you?

Ideally before capital is committed, when requirements and acceptance criteria are still being set. We can also join at tender, during delivery, when a major variation lands, or before acceptance.

Which sectors do you work in?

Manufacturing and process plants, critical commercial operations, logistics and data-intensive sites, and agribusiness infrastructure such as packhouses, cold stores, dairies, producer cellars and irrigation-linked operations.
