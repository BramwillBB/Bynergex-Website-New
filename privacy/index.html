Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Bynergex Consulting (Pty) Ltd ("Bynergex", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information bynergex.com collects, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

Bynergex Consulting (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa, is the responsible party. Our Information Officer is Bramwill Bruinders. Contact him at consultant@bynergex.com or +27 66 252 2655.

What we collect, and why

  • Enquiry form: Your name, email, and optionally your company and phone number, with your message. We use it only to reply to your enquiry and, if you appoint us, to deliver the work. It is sent to our mailbox and is not stored on the website.
  • Downloads: Your name and email, and optionally your company and role. We use it to email you the download, and we keep a record of the request. If you tick the box for occasional updates, we may also email you new guides; you can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
  • Questions on articles: Your name, email and question. Nothing is published unless we approve it. If we do, your name and question appear on the article with our answer, and we email you the answer. Your email address is never published.
  • Ratings: When you rate an article, we store the rating with a scrambled code made from your connection details, so each visitor counts once. We can't identify you from it. Your own rating is remembered in your browser so you can see it next time.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Where it is kept

The website and its records are hosted by Hostinger, whose servers may be located outside South Africa. Emails are held in our Hostinger mailbox. We rely on the provider's safeguards and our own access controls to keep the information secure, as POPIA requires for cross-border transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and download records for as long as they are useful for our relationship with you, and we delete them on request unless the law requires us to keep them. Published questions stay on the article until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop using it for direct marketing. Email consultant@bynergex.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

We may update this policy when our website or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
